I am a Graduate Research Assistant at the Quantum Engineering Lab (led by Prof. Zheshen Zhang) at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, where I am pursuing my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering (GPA: 4.0/4.0).

My research interests lie in quantum information, photonic chip design, and variational quantum algorithms. The main theme of my work is bridging software optimization with experimental atomic physics. Currently, I focus on conducting high-fidelity photonics and quantum chip simulations using Tidy3D to optimize device performance, as well as managing the full hardware testing pipeline from setup to data acquisition.

Previously, I was a Research Assistant at the Institute of Quantum Electronics at Peking University working with Prof. Jingbiao Chen on 420 nm-pumped hot-rubidium vapor experiments. I also spent time as a Research Intern in Prof. Xiao Yuan's group at PKU, developing Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQA) and neural-network quantum states using PyTorch. I obtained my B.E. in Computer Science from Pennsylvania State University.

Research

Photonic & Quantum Chip Simulation

University of Michigan · Quantum Engineering Lab

Conducting high-fidelity photonics and quantum chip simulations using Tidy3D (FDTD solver) to optimize device performance parameters including insertion loss, coupling efficiency, and mode confinement. Managing the full hardware testing pipeline from chip setup to data acquisition, and processing experimental results with Python to identify key performance metrics for iterative hardware design.

Dual-Wavelength Mirrorless Laser in Hot Rubidium Vapor

Peking University · Institute of Quantum Electronics

Conducted 420 nm-pumped hot-rubidium vapor experiments toward realizing a dual-wavelength mirrorless laser. Performed free-space optical alignment and system calibration (polarization control, beam delivery), and implemented pump frequency stabilization using Modulation Transfer Spectroscopy (MTS).

Variational Quantum Algorithms & Neural-Network Quantum States

Peking University · Prof. Xiao Yuan's Group

Developed and optimized Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQA) and neural-network quantum states using PyTorch. Implemented Restricted Boltzmann Machine (RBM) models for quantum state approximation and conducted Variational Monte Carlo (VMC) simulations to study quantum many-body systems.
